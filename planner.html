Transfer Rules Cheat Sheet

Free Transfers

You get 1 free transfer per gameweek. Unused transfers now roll over up to a maximum of 5 saved transfers.

Transfer Hits

Each additional transfer beyond your free quota costs -4 points, deducted directly from your total score.

Wildcard Chip

Activating your Wildcard allows unlimited transfers without taking any point penalties for that gameweek.
